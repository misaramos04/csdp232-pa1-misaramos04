Find Your Place to Focus

Every student studies differently. Some people need silence; others think best with a little background noise. This guide lists campus spots by noise level, hours, and power outlets so you can pick the right place for the work in front of you.

Featured Spots

Quiet Floor Reading Room

Silent study with large tables and natural light.

Best for: exam review and reading

Student Center Lounge

Soft chairs, a coffee counter, and steady background noise.

Best for: flash cards and light homework

Science Building Atrium

Round tables near whiteboards, open late on weeknights.

Best for: team project meetings
