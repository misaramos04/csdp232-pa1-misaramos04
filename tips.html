Five Habits for Better Study Sessions

  1. Decide what you will finish before you sit down.
  2. Work in 25-minute blocks with 5-minute breaks.
  3. Put your phone out of reach, not just face down.
  4. Explain the idea out loud, as if teaching a classmate.
  5. End each session by writing the first step for next time.
